# A/B Experimentation & Conversion Funnel Evaluation

## Overview

This project evaluates the effectiveness of a new e-commerce customer experience using **A/B experimentation and conversion funnel analysis**.

The analysis compares two experiment groups:

- **Control** — Existing customer experience
- **Variant** — New customer experience

The objective is to determine whether the Variant improves customer conversion, identify where customers drop off in the purchasing journey, evaluate statistical significance, and understand the impact on revenue.

---

## Business Problem

An e-commerce company is considering replacing its existing customer experience with a new Variant.

Before rolling out the Variant to all users, the business needs to determine:

- Where customers drop off in the conversion funnel
- Whether the Variant improves purchase conversion
- Whether the difference between Control and Variant is statistically significant
- Whether improved conversion translates into better revenue outcomes
- Which customer segments respond better to the Variant

The analysis ultimately supports a data-driven decision on whether the Variant should be rolled out.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze the customer conversion funnel from **View → Click → Add to Cart → Checkout → Purchase**
- Compare funnel performance between Control and Variant
- Measure purchase conversion and conversion uplift
- Conduct statistical A/B testing
- Evaluate revenue and revenue per user
- Identify meaningful segment-level differences
- Develop a Power BI dashboard to communicate the findings
- Provide a business recommendation based on the analytical results

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains **100,000 users** and includes customer characteristics, experiment assignment, funnel activity, and revenue information.

### Columns

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| `user_id` | Unique identifier for each user |
| `experiment_group` | Experiment group: Control or Variant |
| `device` | Device used by the customer |
| `traffic_source` | Source through which the customer arrived |
| `age_group` | Customer age segment |
| `view` | Indicates whether the customer viewed the experience |
| `click` | Indicates whether the customer clicked |
| `add_to_cart` | Indicates whether the customer added a product to cart |
| `checkout` | Indicates whether the customer reached checkout |
| `purchase` | Indicates whether the customer completed a purchase |
| `order_value` | Value of the customer's order |
| `revenue` | Revenue generated by the customer |
